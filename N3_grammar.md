# N3 grammar study supplement — 80 original entries

Prepared 27 October 2026. **These explanations, Japanese examples, and translations are original supplemental teaching content. They were not extracted from 20 examination papers, are not an official syllabus, and are not ranked by past-paper frequency.** The selection includes intermediate constructions and essential earlier foundations worth consolidating for N3.

JLPT does not publish a post-2010 exhaustive grammar specification list. The two official workbooks are selected practice material, not annual examination releases. See [the official FAQ](https://www.jlpt.jp/e/faq/index.html) and [the official workbook index](https://www.jlpt.jp/e/samples/sampleindex.html).

## How to read the forms

- **V** = verb; **N** = noun; **い-adj** = い-adjective; **な-adj** = な-adjective.
- **Dictionary form**: 書く / 食べる / する. **Stem** removes ます: 書き / 食べ / し.
- **Plain form** includes dictionary, ない, た, and なかった forms. Where nouns or な-adjectives require な, の, or だ, that connection is given explicitly.
- Examples illustrate one common meaning. A pattern can have other uses, and register or context can change the best translation.
- **O18** marks a limited observation in the retrieved 2018 official workbook grammar PDF. It never means “appeared in the 2018 examination.” Exact provenance is listed at the end. All examples remain original.

## 1. Time, sequence, and ongoing situations

| # | Pattern and connection | Meaning and usage | Original example and translation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | **〜間**: Vている / い-adj / な-adjな / Nの + 間 | Throughout a period; the main situation generally continues over that interval. Compare 間に for an event occurring within it. | 母が出かけている間、私は家で犬の世話をした。 — I looked after the dog at home while my mother was out. |
| 02 | **〜間に**: same connections as 間 | During an available interval, something happens or is completed. The main event need not last throughout the interval. | 電車を待っている間に、友達に返事を書いた。 — I wrote a reply to my friend while waiting for the train. |
| 03 | **〜うちに**: V dictionary/ない/ている / い-adj / な-adjな / Nの | Do something before the present condition changes; also describes change occurring unnoticed during an activity. | スープが温かいうちに飲んでください。 — Please drink the soup while it is still warm. |
| 04 | **〜最中に**: Vている / Nの + 最中に | Right in the middle of an activity. Often introduces an interruption; not simply any point in a long period. | 会議の最中に、急に電気が消えた。 — The lights suddenly went out in the middle of the meeting. |
| 05 | **〜ところだ**: V dictionary / Vている / Vた | Distinguishes about to do, currently doing, and just finished. The verb form carries the stage. | 今から夕食を作るところです。 — I am just about to make dinner. |
| 06 | **〜たところ**: Vた + ところ | Having done something, one discovers a result. This use introduces what actually happened, rather than a hypothetical condition. | 店に電話したところ、まだ席があると言われた。 — When I called the restaurant, I was told there were still seats. |
| 07 | **〜たばかりだ**: Vた + ばかりだ | Recently did something, from the speaker's perspective. “Recent” can be minutes or months; unlike たところ, it need not be immediately after completion. | 先月この町に引っ越してきたばかりです。 — I only moved to this town last month. |
| 08 | **〜て以来**: Vて + 以来 | Since an initiating event, a condition or repeated practice has continued. Usually needs a meaningful span after the event. | この本を読んで以来、毎日日記を書くようになった。 — Since reading this book, I have started keeping a diary every day. |
| 09 | **〜たびに**: V dictionary / Nの + たびに | Every time the specified event happens. Links repeated occasions with a repeated result. | この写真を見るたびに、祖父を思い出す。 — Every time I see this photograph, I remember my grandfather. |
| 10 | **〜ついでに**: V dictionary/た / Nの + ついでに | Take the opportunity presented by a main errand to do an additional action. The extra action is secondary. | 郵便局へ行くついでに、牛乳を買ってきます。 — While I am out going to the post office, I will buy some milk. |

## 2. Cause, concession, and contrast

| # | Pattern and connection | Meaning and usage | Original example and translation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 11 | **〜おかげで**: V plain / い-adj / な-adjな / Nの | Thanks to a cause, usually a welcome outcome. Can be sarcastic, but learn the positive use first. | 友達が手伝ってくれたおかげで、準備が早く終わった。 — Thanks to my friend's help, the preparations finished early. |
| 12 | **〜せいで**: same connections as おかげで | Attributes an undesirable result to a cause; often implies blame. せいか softens it to a possible cause. | 昨夜あまり寝なかったせいで、今日は集中できない。 — I cannot concentrate today because I did not sleep much last night. |
| 13 | **〜ために**: V plain / い-adj / な-adjな / Nの | Cause in factual or formal reporting. Separately, V dictionary + ために expresses a deliberate purpose; use context to distinguish them. | 大雪のために、午前中の授業は休みになった。 — Morning classes were cancelled because of heavy snow. |
| 14 | **〜ので**: V/い-adj plain; N/な-adj + なので | Gives an explanatory reason, often with a softer interpersonal tone than a direct から statement. This is a foundation pattern, not uniquely N3. | 道が混んでいるので、少し遅れます。 — I will be a little late because the roads are congested. |
| 15 | **〜のに**: V/い-adj plain; N/な-adj + なのに | An outcome conflicts with expectation. Often conveys surprise, disappointment, or criticism; distinguish from purpose/use のに. | 何度も説明したのに、まだ伝わっていない。 — Although I explained it many times, the message still has not got across. |
| 16 | **〜ても**: Vて + も; い-adjくても; N/な-adjでも | Even if or even though. The main result holds despite the condition; it need not express the speaker's disappointment. | 雨が降っても、試合は行われます。 — The match will take place even if it rains. |
| 17 | **〜くせに**: V plain / い-adj / な-adjな / Nの | Critical “even though,” often accusing someone of inconsistency. Stronger and less neutral than のに; avoid it in polite complaints. | 彼は地図を持っているくせに、見ようとしない。 — Even though he has a map, he will not try to look at it. |
| 18 | **〜わりに**: V plain / い-adj / な-adjな / Nの | An outcome is surprising relative to a standard: “considering...” Usually less accusatory than くせに. | このかばんは値段が安いわりに、丈夫だ。 — This bag is sturdy considering how cheap it is. |
| 19 | **〜代わりに**: V plain / Nの; な-adjな where applicable | Instead of an action/person, or a compensating trade-off. Decide from context whether it is substitution or balance. | 今日は私が料理する代わりに、弟が皿を洗う。 — Today I will cook, and in return my younger brother will wash the dishes. |
| 20 | **〜一方で**: V plain / い-adj / な-adjな / Nである | Presents another side of the same situation, often contrasting advantages and disadvantages. Can also express simultaneous circumstances. | この仕事は忙しい一方で、新しいことを学べる。 — This job is busy, but it also lets me learn new things. |

## 3. Conditions, advice, and hypothetical situations

| # | Pattern and connection | Meaning and usage | Original example and translation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 21 | **〜と**: V dictionary/ない; adjective plain; N/な-adjだ | A regular, automatic, or predictable result. For a simple future conditional, commands and invitations in the result usually require a different conditional. | このボタンを押すと、画面が明るくなります。 — When you press this button, the screen becomes brighter. |
| 22 | **〜ば**: V conditional; い-adjければ; N/な-adjなら(ば) | Highlights a condition needed for a result. Volitional results are possible, but restrictions depend on the first clause's subject and type of action. | 時間があれば、図書館に寄りたい。 — If I have time, I would like to stop at the library. |
| 23 | **〜たら**: plain past + ら | A flexible “if/when,” often with the first event completed before the second. Suitable for requests and intentions. | 駅に着いたら、電話してください。 — Please call me when you arrive at the station. |
| 24 | **〜なら**: V/い-adj plain; N/な-adj directly + なら | Takes up a premise: “if that is the case / if you mean...” Advice in the result may concern something done before the premise occurs. | 京都へ行くなら、先に宿を予約したほうがいい。 — If you are going to Kyoto, you should book accommodation first. |
| 25 | **〜としたら**: plain clause + としたら; retain だ for present N/な-adj | Suppose the stated situation were true. Makes the assumption explicit rather than asserting it. **O18** | 一週間休みが取れるとしたら、どこへ行きたいですか。 — If you could take a week off, where would you like to go? |
| 26 | **〜ばいい**: conditional + いい | Gives a possible solution or asks what to do. Depending on tone, it can sound dismissive; polite forms soften advice. | 分からない言葉は、辞書で調べればいいですよ。 — You can look up words you do not understand in a dictionary. |
| 27 | **〜ばよかった**: conditional + よかった | Regret about an unrealized action; a negative conditional expresses regret about doing something. | 出かける前に、天気予報を見ればよかった。 — I should have checked the weather forecast before going out. |
| 28 | **〜ないと / 〜なければ** with an omitted result | In context, can mean “must,” with a result such as “it will be a problem” left unsaid. The full form is a negative condition, not inherently an obligation. | もう九時だ。そろそろ出ないと。 — It is already nine. I need to leave soon. |
| 29 | **たとえ〜ても** | Even under the assumed, possibly extreme condition, the result will not change. Use でも after nouns and な-adjectives. | たとえ反対されても、自分の考えを説明したい。 — Even if people oppose me, I want to explain my thinking. |
| 30 | **〜ば〜ほど** | The more one factor increases, the more another does. Repeat the verb or adjective: 読めば読むほど; 高ければ高いほど. | 練習すればするほど、話すのが楽しくなる。 — The more I practice, the more enjoyable speaking becomes. |

## 4. Appearance, information, and reasoning

| # | Pattern and connection | Meaning and usage | Original example and translation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 31 | **〜ようだ**: V plain / い-adj / な-adjな / Nの | An inference based on evidence, or a resemblance. ような modifies a noun; ように modifies an action or quality. | 隣の部屋は静かだ。もう誰もいないようだ。 — The next room is quiet. It seems that nobody is there anymore. |
| 32 | **〜みたいだ**: V/い-adj plain; N/な-adj directly | Conversational appearance or resemblance; broadly parallels ようだ but uses different noun connections. Avoid it when formal writing is expected. | あの雲は大きな魚みたいだ。 — That cloud looks like a large fish. |
| 33 | **〜らしい**: V/い-adj plain; N/な-adj directly | Reports or infers something from information. Separately, Nらしい can mean “characteristic of N”; do not merge those meanings automatically. | 新しい図書館は日曜日も開いているらしい。 — Apparently, the new library is open on Sundays too. |
| 34 | **〜そうだ — reported information**: plain clause + そうだ | Relays what a source says. Present nouns and な-adjectives keep だ: 学生だそうだ. This connection differs from appearance そうだ. | 市の案内によると、来月公園が広くなるそうだ。 — According to the city notice, the park will be expanded next month. |
| 35 | **〜そうだ — appearance**: V stem / い-adj minus い / な-adj + そうだ | Looks likely or appears to have a quality. Exceptions include いい→よさそう and ない→なさそう. Do not attach directly to an ordinary noun. | 空が暗くなってきた。雨が降りそうだ。 — The sky has become dark. It looks as though it will rain. |
| 36 | **〜はずだ**: V plain / い-adj / な-adjな / Nの | An expectation supported by what the speaker knows. It is not simply a future wish or obligation. | 今日は祝日だから、銀行は休みのはずだ。 — Since today is a public holiday, the bank should be closed. |
| 37 | **〜はずがない**: same connections as はずだ | Strongly rejects an outcome as inconsistent with known circumstances. Usually stronger than a tentative “probably not.” | まだ店が開く時間ではないから、客がいるはずがない。 — It is not opening time yet, so there cannot be customers inside. |
| 38 | **〜わけだ**: V plain / い-adj / な-adjな / Nな・である | Explains why something follows logically: “that explains it / so...” Often expresses realization after new information. | この道は駅への近道なのか。人が多いわけだ。 — So this road is a shortcut to the station. That explains why there are so many people. |
| 39 | **〜わけがない**: same connections as わけだ | Rejects a possibility emphatically: “there is no way...” Often reflects the speaker's reasoning or conviction. | 一日でこの厚い本を全部覚えられるわけがない。 — There is no way I can memorize this whole thick book in one day. |
| 40 | **〜わけではない**: same connections as わけだ | Limits a claim: “it does not mean that / not necessarily.” Often a partial denial, not a complete opposite assertion. | 甘い物が嫌いなわけではないが、今日は食べたくない。 — It is not that I dislike sweets; I just do not want any today. |

## 5. Decisions, habits, goals, and attempts

| # | Pattern and connection | Meaning and usage | Original example and translation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 41 | **〜ことにする**: V dictionary/ない + ことにする | The subject decides on an action. ことにしている can describe a personal rule maintained over time. | 夜は携帯電話を机に置いて寝ることにした。 — I decided to leave my phone on the desk when I go to bed. |
| 42 | **〜ことになる**: V dictionary/ない + ことになる | An arrangement or development results in an action. The expression backgrounds personal choice; it need not mean someone forcibly decided for the speaker. | 来月から別の部署で働くことになった。 — It has been arranged that I will work in another department from next month. |
| 43 | **〜ことになっている**: V dictionary/ない + ことになっている | An established rule, arrangement, or schedule. Distinguish this from ことにしている, a personal practice. | この建物では、入口で名前を書くことになっています。 — In this building, visitors are required to write their names at the entrance. |
| 44 | **〜ように — purpose**: V dictionary/ない + ように | Aims for a condition, ability, or prevention; often uses potential verbs. Compare ために for a deliberate action performed toward a goal. | 後ろの人にも聞こえるように、大きな声で話した。 — I spoke loudly so that the people at the back could hear too. |
| 45 | **〜ようになる**: V dictionary/ない + ようになる | A change in ability, state, or habitual behavior. Frequently appears with potential verbs. | 最近、字幕なしでも少し分かるようになった。 — Recently, I have become able to understand a little even without subtitles. |
| 46 | **〜ようにする**: V dictionary/ない + ようにする | Make a conscious effort to establish or maintain behavior. ようにしている emphasizes ongoing effort or habit. | 約束の時間に遅れないようにしています。 — I make a point of not being late for appointments. |
| 47 | **〜つもりだ**: V dictionary/ない + つもりだ | A settled intention. Vた + つもり can instead describe one's belief that an action was completed; it is not always future intention. | 週末は家でゆっくり休むつもりです。 — I intend to have a relaxing rest at home this weekend. |
| 48 | **〜ことはない**: V dictionary + ことはない | There is no need to do something; often reassurance. With different tense/context, related wording may describe absence of occasions, so read the whole sentence. | 小さな失敗だから、そんなに心配することはない。 — It is a small mistake, so there is no need to worry so much. |
| 49 | **〜べきだ**: V dictionary + べきだ; する→するべき/すべき | What ought to be done on principle or as a duty. Stronger than a casual suggestion; avoid using it bluntly toward someone senior. | 借りた物は、約束の日までに返すべきだ。 — You should return borrowed things by the agreed date. |
| 50 | **〜ようとする**: V volitional + とする | Attempts an action, or is just about to act. ようとしない often describes unwillingness to try. | ドアを開けようとしたが、鍵がかかっていた。 — I tried to open the door, but it was locked. |

## 6. Topic, viewpoint, role, and relationship

| # | Pattern and connection | Meaning and usage | Original example and translation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 51 | **〜について**: N + について | About a topic of discussion, study, or thought. Before another noun, use についての. | 授業で、日本の交通について調べています。 — In class, we are researching transport in Japan. |
| 52 | **〜に関して**: N + に関して | Concerning a topic; generally more formal than について. に関する modifies a noun. | 申し込みに関して質問があれば、受付に聞いてください。 — If you have questions concerning applications, please ask at reception. |
| 53 | **〜に対して**: N + に対して | Toward a recipient or target, or in contrast with another item. Do not automatically replace it with topic-marking について. | 店員は客に対して、丁寧に説明した。 — The employee explained things politely to the customer. |
| 54 | **〜にとって**: N + にとって | From someone's standpoint, usually followed by an evaluation such as important, difficult, or useful. It does not mark the recipient of an action. | 私にとって、朝の散歩は大切な時間だ。 — For me, my morning walk is an important part of the day. |
| 55 | **〜として**: N + として | In the role or capacity of N; としての modifies a following noun. **O18** | 姉は通訳として、この会議に参加した。 — My older sister took part in this conference as an interpreter. |
| 56 | **〜によって**: N + によって | Depending on, by means of, because of, or by an agent in some passive sentences. Choose the meaning from the noun and predicate. | この料理の作り方は、地域によって違う。 — The way this dish is made differs by region. |
| 57 | **〜によると**: N + によると | Identifies an information source; often followed by reported そうだ, らしい, or a quotation. It does not express the cause of an event. | 天気予報によると、午後は晴れるそうです。 — According to the forecast, it will be sunny in the afternoon. |
| 58 | **〜と比べて / 〜に比べて**: N + either form | Compared with a reference point. In this common comparative use, both forms can be possible; do not count them automatically as separate concepts. | 去年と比べて、今年は雨が少ない。 — Compared with last year, there has been less rain this year. |
| 59 | **〜にしたがって**: V dictionary / N + にしたがって | As one process changes, another changes accordingly. With nouns such as instructions, it can instead mean “in accordance with.” | 山を登るにしたがって、空気が冷たくなった。 — As we climbed the mountain, the air became colder. |
| 60 | **〜につれて**: V dictionary / N + につれて | As one development progresses, another changes with it. The linked development is usually natural or progressive, rather than a one-off command. | 日が短くなるにつれて、朝も寒くなってきた。 — As the days have grown shorter, the mornings have become colder too. |

## 7. Limits, degree, addition, and absence

| # | Pattern and connection | Meaning and usage | Original example and translation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 61 | **〜だけ**: N / V plain + だけ | Only, just, or as much as a specified limit permits. Unlike しか, it does not require a negative predicate. | 今日は資料を読むだけで、発表はしません。 — Today we will only read the material; we will not give presentations. |
| 62 | **〜しか〜ない**: N or quantity + しか + negative | Only, with emphasis on a restricted amount. The Japanese predicate is negative even when English uses an affirmative “only.” | 財布には千円しか入っていない。 — There is only one thousand yen in my wallet. |
| 63 | **〜ばかり**: N + ばかり; Vて + ばかりいる | Nothing but / repeatedly doing the same thing, often with disapproval. Different from たばかり, meaning recently completed. | 弟は休みの日、ゲームをしてばかりいる。 — On his days off, my younger brother does nothing but play games. |
| 64 | **〜だけでなく〜も**: N / plain clause + だけでなく; な-adjな | Not only the first item but also another. Align the grammatical roles of the two items. | この店は安いだけでなく、駅からも近い。 — This shop is not only inexpensive but also close to the station. |
| 65 | **〜ほど〜ない**: N + ほど + negative predicate | Not as much/as ... as the comparison point. Reversing the two nouns reverses the comparison. | 今日は昨日ほど寒くない。 — Today is not as cold as yesterday. |
| 66 | **〜くらい / 〜ぐらい**: quantity, N, or clause | Approximate quantity, illustrative degree, or a modest minimum. Meaning depends on context; do not translate every use as “about.” | 声が聞こえないくらい、雨の音が大きかった。 — The rain was so loud that I could not hear the voice. |
| 67 | **〜さえ**: N + さえ; particles may precede it | Even an unexpected or extreme item. In さえ〜ば, it can identify the sole sufficient condition: “as long as.” | 忙しくて、昼ご飯を食べる時間さえなかった。 — I was so busy that I did not even have time to eat lunch. |
| 68 | **〜でも — representative suggestion**: N + でも | Suggests one example among alternatives, often casually. This is different from conditional ても/でも. | 少し休んで、お茶でも飲みませんか。 — Shall we take a short break and have some tea or something? |
| 69 | **〜まで — unexpected inclusion**: N + まで | Even an additional, surprising item. Separate this from the ordinary endpoint meaning “until/as far as.” | 今日は普段静かな田中さんまで笑っていた。 — Today even Tanaka, who is usually quiet, was laughing. |
| 70 | **〜ずに**: negative base + ずに; する→せずに | Without doing an action; often more written or formal than ないで. It is not formed by adding ず to the complete ない form. | 朝ご飯を食べずに、家を出てしまった。 — I left home without eating breakfast. |

## 8. Aspect, voice, and polite interaction

| # | Pattern and connection | Meaning and usage | Original example and translation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 71 | **〜てある**: transitive Vて + ある | A resulting state left by someone's intentional action. Often places focus on the prepared object; distinguish from an intransitive verb plus ている. | 机の上に、明日の資料が並べてある。 — Tomorrow's materials have been laid out on the desk. |
| 72 | **〜ておく**: Vて + おく | Do something in preparation, or leave a condition as it is. Spoken ておく often contracts to とく. | 忘れないように、予定を手帳に書いておいた。 — I wrote the appointment in my planner beforehand so I would not forget. |
| 73 | **〜てしまう**: Vて + しまう | Completion, or an action perceived as unfortunate/unintended. Regret is contextual, not present in every use. Spoken forms include ちゃう/じゃう. | 大切なメモを捨ててしまった。 — I accidentally threw away an important note. |
| 74 | **〜ていく**: Vて + いく | Movement away from a reference point, or development continuing from now into the future. Identify whether the context is spatial or temporal. | これからも日本語の勉強を続けていきたい。 — I want to continue studying Japanese from now on. |
| 75 | **〜てくる**: Vて + くる | Movement toward the reference point, a change developing up to now, or onset becoming noticeable. Sometimes means going to do something and returning. | 最近、朝早く起きるのに慣れてきた。 — Recently, I have been getting used to waking up early. |
| 76 | **Passive**: godan a-row + れる; ichidan stem + られる; する→される; 来る→来られる | The subject receives an action; Japanese also has affected/adversity passives. With ichidan verbs, distinguish passive, potential, and honorific られる through context. **O18** | この橋は五十年前に建てられた。 — This bridge was built fifty years ago. |
| 77 | **Causative**: godan a-row + せる; ichidan stem + させる; する→させる; 来る→来させる | Make or let someone do something; context determines compulsion versus permission. The person caused to act is often に with transitive verbs, while particle choices vary with verb type. | 先生は学生に短い作文を書かせた。 — The teacher had the students write a short composition. |
| 78 | **Causative passive**: causative form made passive | Be made to do something. 書く→書かせられる, often 書かされる; 食べる→食べさせられる. Do not apply the short godan form mechanically to every verb. | 子どものころ、毎日野菜を食べさせられた。 — When I was a child, I was made to eat vegetables every day. |
| 79 | **お + V stem + ください**; **ご + suitable Sino-Japanese action noun + ください** | Respectful requests. The construction is lexical: not every verb naturally accepts お, and some verbs use special honorific forms. **O18** | こちらにお名前をお書きください。 — Please write your name here. |
| 80 | **Humble/polite self-reference: 申す, いたす, 伺う, いただく** | Learn each verb's function: 申す for one's saying/name, いたす for one's doing, 伺う for visiting/asking, いただく for receiving/eating or receiving a favor. Do not use humble verbs to elevate someone else's action. **O18: 申します only** | 私は森と申します。本日はよろしくお願いいたします。 — My name is Mori. I look forward to working with you today. |

## Comparison notes that prevent common mistakes

1. **間 / 間に**: “I stayed home throughout the holiday” favors 間; “I finished the report during the holiday” favors 間に.
2. **たところ / たばかり**: The former can locate an action immediately after completion; the latter describes subjective recency. Entry 06 separately covers the “did it and discovered...” construction.
3. **ので / のに**: One explains why the result makes sense; the other marks a result that goes against expectation. Check whether the two clauses agree causally before considering their English translations.
4. **と / ば / たら / なら**: First identify whether you need a regular consequence, a necessary condition, a completed event before another, or advice based on an assumed situation. These are overlapping tools, not four interchangeable translations of “if.”
5. **Appearance そう / reported そう**: 雨が降りそうだ is a prediction from appearance; 雨が降るそうだ relays information. The verb forms differ.
6. **はず / わけ**: はず gives an expectation from existing knowledge; explanatory わけ connects an observed fact to its explanation. Their negative forms can overlap, but context still matters.
7. **ことにする / ことになる**: The former foregrounds a decision by the subject; the latter presents an arrangement or outcome. They do not necessarily reveal who made the decision in real life.
8. **ために / ように**: Deliberate purpose commonly uses ために; a desired ability or condition commonly uses ように. Compare “save to buy a car” with “speak clearly so everyone can hear.”
9. **について / に対して / にとって / として**: Topic, target, viewpoint, and role are different relationships. Identify the noun's role before choosing the expression.
10. **ている / てある / ておく**: A state or ongoing action, a deliberately produced result, and a preparatory action are different perspectives. Also inspect whether the verb is transitive or intransitive.
