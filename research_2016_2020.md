# JLPT N3 source audit: July/December 2016–2020

Checked 27 October 2026. This is a source-discovery audit, not a collection of exam papers or an extracted vocabulary/grammar corpus. Searches used English, Japanese, Vietnamese, and Chinese. No full exam was downloaded or reproduced, and no claim of exhaustive internet coverage is made.

## Principal findings

- **July 2020 was cancelled worldwide.** The Japan Foundation's announcement explicitly covers every overseas testing site and reports cancellation in Japan. There is no administered July 2020 N3 paper to collect. [Official cancellation announcement](https://www.jpf.go.jp/e/about/press/2020/001.html)
- **December 2020 did take place.** The official statistics report 98,208 N3 examinees across Japan and overseas. Thus local December cancellations must not be mistaken for a worldwide cancellation. [Official December 2020 statistics](https://www.jlpt.jp/e/statistics/archive/202002.html)
- Accessible third-party session listings exist for the nine remaining slots. They do **not** establish official publication, a license, authenticity, or completeness. A filename or session heading alone is insufficient evidence.
- The former archive at De Thi Tieng Nhat now says it has stopped publishing official exam questions to comply with Japan Foundation copyright policy and instead provides newly created mock tests. Historical recommendations to that site are stale for past-paper retrieval. [Current N3 page and notice](https://dethitiengnhat.com/en/jlpt/N3)

## Access and completeness notes

- Vietnamese-localized JLPT Practice Test session URLs returned cache-miss errors in this research browser. The corresponding English URLs shown in the table all opened. This is a tool-observed fetch difference, not proof that the Vietnamese URLs are universally broken.
- The JLPT Practice Test N3 index describes the historical listings as free text sets. Its listening materials are separately described as site-created mock tests, so combining those mocks with dated text sets would misrepresent historical listening coverage. [N3 index](https://www.jlptpracticetest.com/vi/n3)
- **No entry is ready to support “each and every vocabulary word and grammar structure from the authentic full exam.”** That claim requires complete verified question text, all options, reading passages, listening prompts and scripts, a defined treatment of inflections/names/function words, and an occurrence-to-source audit. This source search establishes candidates and gaps; it does not perform that extraction.
- Within these ten scheduled slots, the defensible inventory is **nine administered-session candidates plus one cancelled slot**. Full-text and listening verification remain outstanding; no official year-and-session released paper was authenticated by this audit.
