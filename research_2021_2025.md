# JLPT N3 session-source audit: July and December 2021–2025

Checked 27 October 2026. This is a source inventory, not a collection of authenticated examination papers. Search used English, Vietnamese, Chinese and Japanese terms. Every linked source below was opened; verification concerns the visible landing page unless stated otherwise. An accessible landing page does **not** establish that its linked PDF is complete, its audio plays, its answers are correct, or its reproduction is authorized.

No official session-specific release was established by this research. All third-party rights and authenticity remain unverified. No full papers, scans, answer keys or transcripts are reproduced here, and no direct scan/file-download links are included. “Not found” means not verified in this audit, not proof of nonexistence.

## Source assessment and evidence locations

- **Kosei**: session subsections appear in the opened page's lines 478–569. Many entries credit Yuuki Bùi. December 2023 explicitly says it is reference material rather than an official paper. General full-answer access uses a form. These observations verify labels, not the underlying files. [Source][kosei]
- **JLPTPracticeTest**: its index lists both sessions for 2021–2024 and describes vocabulary, grammar and reading content, independently republished and unaffiliated with JLPT organizers. The July 2021 and December 2024 session pages were opened and have separate text-section menus. This is not evidence of session listening material or printable complete papers. Its separate listening practice products must not be merged into the dated sets. [Index][jptindex]
- **HanoiLink**: the actual table lists both sessions of 2022–2024; its 2025 row still says “updating.” Its broad 2022–2025 title therefore overstates verified 2025 availability. The page also contains internally inconsistent score guidance, so use official sources for exam rules. [Source][hanoi]
- **TNDG, December 2021 and December 2024**: the titles advertise paper, answers and listening; the opened bodies expose an answers/script panel with an embedded frame. Neither frame contents nor playback were validated. [2021][tndg202112], [2024][tndg202412]
- **TNDG, July 2025**: the title promises answers; visible panel contains an answer image, and a reader asks about reading/listening. A reusable button labelled answers and script does not prove a transcript exists. [Source][tndg202507]
- **TNDG, December 2025**: visible panel has three answer images and a download label. No separately identified audio or transcript appeared in the opened extraction. [Source][tndg202512]
- **Tiếng Nhật Cơ Bản, July 2025**: page is dated 6 July 2025 and has an embedded Drive frame, a PDF label and an answer image. This verifies a candidate landing page, not legitimacy, completeness or the linked file. [Source][tncb202507]
- **Nam Triều, July 2025**: N3 tables are explicitly reference answers. Numeric tables cover all four skills but supply neither the questions nor listening recordings. [Source][nam202507]
- **Tiếng Nhật Hay, December 2025**: visible numeric reference keys cover all four skills. Some reading question-range labels do not match the number of numbers shown, an editing inconsistency; accuracy should not be assumed. [Source][tnh202512]
- **Mison, July 2021**: Chinese reference-answer page dated 5 July 2021, with four separately labelled answer images. It is answer-only evidence, not a paper/audio collection. Its score-estimation advertising is not relied upon. [Source][mison202107]
- **Uno Japano**: opened December 2023, 2024 and 2025 answer posts contain images. The latter two explicitly describe reference answers. December 2025 further labels the material Chinese domestic JLPT; this regional label is the site's claim, not an independently established separate official form. [2023][uno202312], [2024][uno202412], [2025][uno202512]

## Useful distinctions: online mocks, catalog claims and failed checks

- [PassJapanese Japanese catalog][passjp] lists both sessions for all five years, but calls them **past-paper-format mock exams**, describes author explanations and VIP access, and explicitly says there are no PDF downloads. It states it is unaffiliated with the organizers. The [December 2025 mock landing page][pass202512] also opens. These are mock-test alternatives, not authenticated session papers; access to all questions, audio and transcripts was not tested.
- [TestJLPT Chinese catalog][testjlpt] visibly lists all ten sessions. Its title uses “official questions,” but the opened catalog only exposed date listings and user/status information; it does not substantiate that marketing claim or full availability.
- Chinese Gaotu search results described 2023 reference answers. December 2023 failed to open; the July 2023 page opened but extracted extensive navigation without the claimed article text. Neither is counted as verified answer content.
- Chinese iFoor search results advertised PDFs, audio, scripts and answer explanations for 2021–2024, but the catalog timed out when opened. Not counted as verified availability.
- Aixin Chinese December 2022 answer-list page also timed out. Not counted as verified.
- Search results also included user-uploaded document platforms, torrent collections and marketplace compilations. They were not used as download sources or treated as authenticated official releases.

## Practical conclusion

There are verified third-party landing-page leads for all ten sessions. The audit establishes **zero authenticated complete session packages**. The strongest recurring component labels are on Kosei for 2021–2024; 2025 has explicit answer-only evidence and paper-labelled leads, but listening audio and full transcripts remain unverified. Use official sample/workbook material as the trustworthy baseline, and label every unofficial session source before using it for study or any frequency analysis.
